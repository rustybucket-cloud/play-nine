How to Play

Goal

Like golf, the lowest score wins. Each hole, your 8 cards score points — make pairs and flip low cards to keep your score down.

Each hole

  • You get 8 face-down cards in two rows of four. Start by flipping any 2.
  • On your turn: draw the top card from the draw pile or take the top discard.
  • Swap the drawn card with any of your 8 cards (the old card goes to the discard) — or, if you drew from the draw pile, discard it and flip one of your face-down cards instead. If you took the discard, you must swap it in.
  • When a player's 8 cards are all face up, the other player gets one last turn, then the hole is scored.

Scoring

  • Cards are worth their face value (0–12).
  • A vertical pair (same value in a column) scores 0.
  • −5 (Hole-in-One) cards score −5 each; a pair of them scores −10.
